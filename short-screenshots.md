# Sample: a vertical short

Sample material for a first 9:16 short (say that it is a sample). Every fact below has its source.

**Topic:** three Mac screenshot shortcuts.

**Hook:** "You only need three."

**The three** — 1 and 2: Apple Support, "Take a screenshot on Mac", https://support.apple.com/102646 · 3: Apple, Mac User Guide, "Take screenshots or screen recordings on Mac", https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh26782/mac (both read 2026-11-18)

1. Shift ⌘ 3 — the whole screen
2. Shift ⌘ 4 — drag to capture a part of it
3. Shift ⌘ 5 — the capture bar: a window, a part, or a recording

**Payoff:** "Shift ⌘ 5 does all of them."
